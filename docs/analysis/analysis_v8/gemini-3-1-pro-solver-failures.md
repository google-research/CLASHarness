> [!NOTE]
> **Scope.** One model, one corpus. Every figure below is
> `reasoning_effort=high` on the v8 conflict corpus (600 tasks = 60 scenarios

gemini-3-1-pro ranks 8 of 9 on all four capability metrics, and that uniformity is the finding. Per-check accuracy is 48.2% against a field median of 58.3%, 1,820 required and diagnostic units fail against a median of 1,489, and 9.8% of tasks pass against a median of 20.0%. ### 4.3 Designed difficulty levers

Sections 4.1 and 4.2 measure difficulty as it turned out. This one measures it
as it was *designed*: each task is joined to the scenario seed it was generated
from, and grouped by what that seed declares. The two boolean knobs give
non-overlapping arms; the conflict plugins do not. The generator resolves each
seed's `conflict_taxonomy` and `difficulty_challenges` tokens against the plugin
registry and one seed routes to several plugins at once, so those rows overlap
and do not sum to the corpus.

Read the `seeds` column first. Some plugins are carried by a single seed, and a
delta on one of those describes that one scenario rather than the lever, however
large it looks. The corpus-wide version of this table, with all thirteen models side

## 5. Interpretation

<!-- PROSE-interpretation-pro-BEGIN -->

At 9.8% pass@budget this is the second-weakest solver in the corpus, and the weakness is structural in the plainest sense: rank 8 of 9 on every capability metric, with two penalties compounding rather than cancelling. Per-check accuracy of 48.2% is the first; the second is that its failures concentrate less than anyone above it, a lift of only 1.19×, so each dropped check costs a whole task more often than it does for the field. The action stream shows where the accuracy goes — `asset_inspect` at 1.77 against a field median of 1.11 and 281 wait actions against output verbs at or below median, `message_send` 2.15 and `file_write` 1.04 — and 61 runs simply exhaust the budget, 41 of them looping on a single verb. Reasoning effort is the one lever that measurably works, +3.7 pp at p = 0.00068, but it pays for that with capped runs rising 28 → 152, which is buying passes with budget rather than with better answers. The bottleneck is not perception: 511 of 541 failed tasks involve a content check and exactly 1 fails on grounding alone, so the headroom is entirely in composing a complete, correct artifact and stopping.
