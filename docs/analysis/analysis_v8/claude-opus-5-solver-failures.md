claude-opus-5 leads the corpus on both task-level metrics while ranking only 2 of 9 on per-check accuracy at 61.7%. A model that scattered the same misses across more tasks would post the same per-check accuracy and pass far less often.

### 3.3 Failure clustering

| clustering statistic | value | rank of 13 | field median |
|---|---:|---:|---:|

| lift | **1.49×** | 1 | 1.36× |
| overdispersion | **2.23×** | 1 | 1.97× |
| lift 95% CI | **[1.39, 1.59]** | | |
| checks / tasks scored | **3,284 / 600** | | |

<!-- SF7-opus5-END -->

<!-- PROSE-clustering-opus5-BEGIN -->

This is the mechanism behind the ranking in section 1. A per-check pass rate of 61.7% would yield only a 9.9% task pass rate if checks failed independently; the observed rate is 25.3%, a lift of 1.48× whose 95% CI of [1.39, 1.57] excludes 1. Failures concentrate — this model tends to be wholly right or wholly wrong about a scenario rather than dropping a check at random — and its overdispersion of 2.28× is the highest in the corpus. That 15.5 pp excess is what converts second place on accuracy into first place on tasks.

### 4.3 Designed difficulty levers

Sections 4.1 and 4.2 measure difficulty as it turned out. This one measures it
as it was *designed*: each task is joined to the scenario seed it was generated
from, and grouped by what that seed declares. The two boolean knobs give
non-overlapping arms; the conflict plugins do not. The generator resolves each
seed's `conflict_taxonomy` and `difficulty_challenges` tokens against the plugin
registry and one seed routes to several plugins at once, so those rows overlap
and do not sum to the corpus.

delta on one of those describes that one scenario rather than the lever, however

## 5. Interpretation

<!-- PROSE-interpretation-opus5-BEGIN -->

The best model in the corpus passes 25.3% of tasks, so the benchmark is nowhere near saturated. Its lead is structural rather than raw: 2 of 9 on per-check accuracy but 1 of 9 on task pass rate, with the 1.48× clustering lift bridging the two. What remains is a production problem rather than a perception problem — grounding is the bucket effort actually fixes (68 → 26), while `side_effect` sits at the field median of 63.1% and `content_msg` at 50.0% remains the largest absolute cost. More deliberation lifts avg_score to 0.6419 without lifting the pass rate, which puts the headroom in emitting a complete, correct artifact rather than in thinking or looking longer.
