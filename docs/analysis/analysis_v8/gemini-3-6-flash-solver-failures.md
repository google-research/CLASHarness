# gemini-3.6-flash — solver failure deep-dive (v8 corpus)

> [!NOTE]
> **Scope.** One model, one corpus. Every figure below is
> `reasoning_effort=high` on the v8 conflict corpus (600 tasks = 60 scenarios
> × 10 rounds) unless a row says otherwise. Cross-model comparisons live in

Effort buys nothing and past medium it costs: pass@budget goes 18.5%, 20.2%, 18.5% while avg_score peaks at medium (0.6023) and at high (0.5857) drops below the low-effort value. The low → high McNemar is 0.0 pp on 34 gained and 34 lost at p = 1: a complete substitution of one set of passes for another, not movement.

### 4.3 Designed difficulty levers

Sections 4.1 and 4.2 measure difficulty as it turned out. This one measures it
as it was *designed*: each task is joined to the scenario seed it was generated
from, and grouped by what that seed declares. The two boolean knobs give
non-overlapping arms; the conflict plugins do not. The generator resolves each
seed's `conflict_taxonomy` and `difficulty_challenges` tokens against the plugin
registry and one seed routes to several plugins at once, so those rows overlap
and do not sum to the corpus.

Read the `seeds` column first. Some plugins are carried by a single seed, and a
delta on one of those describes that one scenario rather than the lever, however
large it looks. The corpus-wide version of this table, with all thirteen models side

## 5. Interpretation

<!-- PROSE-interpretation-flash36-BEGIN -->

On the four headline metrics gemini-3-6-flash is the mid-field reference point, rank 4 of 9 on each and a fraction above the field median on each, but the shape underneath is not the mid-field average. It is not perception-limited: it reads more than the rest of the field, `file_read` at 3.72 per task against 1.95, and posts the second-lowest grounding failure rate at 10.1%. It is production-limited — 87 capped runs, 67 of them single-verb loops, 36 tasks with no write at all, and 408 of 479 failed tasks turning on a content check. Reasoning effort makes this worse rather than better because the extra deliberation is spent inside the same step budget: capped runs go 11 to 148 and pass@budget returns to 18.5% at high. The headroom is termination discipline and artifact production, and the 4.6% fail-closed rate against a field median of 1.0% means part of the score is not a wrong answer marked wrong but an empty one leaving nothing to mark.
