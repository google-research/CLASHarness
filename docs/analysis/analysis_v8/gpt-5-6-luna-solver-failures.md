# gpt-5.6-luna — solver failure deep-dive (v8 corpus)

> [!NOTE]
> **Scope.** One model, one corpus. Every figure below is
> `reasoning_effort=high` on the v8 conflict corpus (600 tasks = 60 scenarios
> × 10 rounds) unless a row says otherwise. Cross-model comparisons live in
> [cross-model-behavioral-comparison.md](./cross-model-behavioral-comparison.md);
> the measurement-validity argument, the F1–F6 definitions and the corpus-wide
> difficulty analysis live in

### 4.3 Designed difficulty levers

Sections 4.1 and 4.2 measure difficulty as it turned out. This one measures it
as it was *designed*: each task is joined to the scenario seed it was generated
from, and grouped by what that seed declares. The two boolean knobs give
non-overlapping arms; the conflict plugins do not. The generator resolves each
seed's `conflict_taxonomy` and `difficulty_challenges` tokens against the plugin
registry and one seed routes to several plugins at once, so those rows overlap
and do not sum to the corpus.

Read the `seeds` column first. Some plugins are carried by a single seed, and a
delta on one of those describes that one scenario rather than the lever, however
large it looks. The corpus-wide version of this table, with all thirteen models side

## 5. Interpretation

<!-- PROSE-interpretation-luna-BEGIN -->

gpt-5-6-luna is the median model of the corpus at check level and sixth of nine at task level, and those are the same fact read from either side of the clustering term: 58.3% accuracy predicts 7.8%, and corpus-typical concentration would have converted that into more than the 9.9 pp of excess it actually earns. It is the weakest of the three models in its family for that reason rather than through any distinctive incompetence — its per-scenario gaps never exceed +0.122 or fall below −0.050, and its near-miss count of 293 is the highest measured. Where it does give up check-level ground it gives it up on the buckets that decide this benchmark, `content_msg` at 56.0% and `anchor` at 26.6%, both rank 7 of 9 and both failures of emission: a message never sent, a literal string missing from a file that was written. Effort does not recover it — low to high moves +0.3 pp at p = 0.89 while capped tasks go 3 to 55 — and the bucket that improves most is grounding, 62 to 36, which the gating breakdown shows is the sole cause of just 5 of 494 failures, while `content_msg` and `side_effect` drift down over the same range without changing the outcome. The headroom is in finishing the artifact with the required strings in it, not in reading, waiting or thinking longer.

<!-- PROSE-interpretation-luna-END -->
