> [!NOTE]
> **Scope.** One model, one corpus. Every figure below is
> `reasoning_effort=high` on the v8 conflict corpus (600 tasks = 60 scenarios
> × 10 rounds) unless a row says otherwise. Cross-model comparisons live in

Grounding is the one bucket where this model is competitive: 13.3% failure, rank 4, slightly under the field median of 13.9%. ### 4.3 Designed difficulty levers

Sections 4.1 and 4.2 measure difficulty as it turned out. This one measures it
as it was *designed*: each task is joined to the scenario seed it was generated
from, and grouped by what that seed declares. The two boolean knobs give
non-overlapping arms; the conflict plugins do not. The generator resolves each
seed's `conflict_taxonomy` and `difficulty_challenges` tokens against the plugin
registry and one seed routes to several plugins at once, so those rows overlap
and do not sum to the corpus.

delta on one of those describes that one scenario rather than the lever, however
large it looks. The corpus-wide version of this table, with all thirteen models side

## 5. Interpretation

<!-- PROSE-interpretation-flash-BEGIN -->

gemini-3-5-flash is the weakest model in the corpus by a wide margin, and the deficit is a control problem rather than a perception problem. Grounding failures sit at 13.3% at rank 4, so the model locates and opens the right assets; it then fails to stop, capping out on 510 tasks and leaving 390 with nothing written, which is why 50.2% of the semantic checks fail closed with nothing to grade. That single mechanism accounts for the rest of the document — the 75.9% `content_file` failure rate, the near-total absence of clustering at 1.12× overdispersion, and a stop-reason table in which the harness rather than the model ends 85.0% of runs. Reasoning effort makes it strictly worse, 0 tasks gained against 63 lost from low to high, because the extra deliberation is spent inside the loop that causes the failure. Reading these numbers as a content-quality gap would be a mistake: until this model terminates and emits, the quality of its content is mostly unobserved, and the corpus-wide finding that body content discriminates cannot be tested on it.

<!-- PROSE-interpretation-flash-END -->
