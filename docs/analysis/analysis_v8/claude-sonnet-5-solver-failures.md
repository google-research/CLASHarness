### 4.3 Designed difficulty levers

Sections 4.1 and 4.2 measure difficulty as it turned out. This one measures it
as it was *designed*: each task is joined to the scenario seed it was generated
from, and grouped by what that seed declares. The two boolean knobs give
non-overlapping arms; the conflict plugins do not. The generator resolves each
seed's `conflict_taxonomy` and `difficulty_challenges` tokens against the plugin
registry and one seed routes to several plugins at once, so those rows overlap
and do not sum to the corpus.

delta on one of those describes that one scenario rather than the lever, however
large it looks. The corpus-wide version of this table, with all thirteen models side

## 5. Interpretation

<!-- PROSE-interpretation-sonnet-BEGIN -->

claude-sonnet-5 sits at 17.0% pass@budget and 7 of 9 on every headline metric, with no gap between its check-level and task-level ranks, so there is no hidden structural advantage waiting to be recovered. Its distinguishing pathology is perceptual rather than compositional: 185 tasks with no asset open, `asset_open` at 1.17 per task against a field median of 2.36, and a grounding failure rate of 35.3% that is last of nine against a field median of 13.9%. That is also the part effort fixes — F3 failures fall 188 → 76 from low to high and pass@budget reaches 20.8% at `p = 1e-07`, a genuine effort response rather than churn — but it is a partial fix, because only 18 failed tasks are lost to grounding alone while 408 of 498 involve a content check. The honest reading is that forcing an asset open would move this model up the field cheaply and then stop: `content_msg` at 641 required failures and `side_effect` at 69.1% are the ceiling, and neither responds the way grounding does: `content_msg` ends at 643 having started at 682, and `side_effect` moves only 357 to 319, while grounding falls by more than half. Even the high-effort configuration at 20.8% only reaches the level the field reaches at medium, so this is a mid-pack model whose most tractable deficit is also its least valuable one.

<!-- PROSE-interpretation-sonnet-END -->
